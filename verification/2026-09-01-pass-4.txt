Escapes and bugs, fixed in the revision commit
----------------------------------------------
- The consent floor compared by clock (`ts >= moved_at`), not by arrival:
  an approval sharing the reversal's second but arriving before it revived
  consent — F418 read `ready` the instant the rollback landed; the aborted
  canary read `ready`. Found by three agents independently; the audit's
  as-of floor had the same hole (an approval arriving after the decision,
  same second, was credited). Fixed: v_intent_moved carries the moving
  decision's seq; v_approval.valid is `seq > moved_seq`; v_audit_ctx
  floors on seq; v_step_term floors on the transition's started_seq.
  Pinned: m_same_second_approval.
- Rollback of a rollback: v_reversal stayed true for F418 after intent
  returned to it, so lanes/trace/release cards said rolled-back while the
  grid (an inline derivation) said pending. Fixed: a reversal holds only
  while intent has not since returned to the freight (moved_seq >
  last_seq), and the grid now reads v_reversal too — the "one derivation"
  smells #39 claimed. Pinned: m_rollback_of_rollback.
- Direction, reversal and cumulative membership ordered freights by
  discovered_at alone; two freights discovered in the same second made a
  rollback read as forward and dropped a PR from membership. Fixed:
  (discovered_at, seq) for direction/reversal/audit; seq for membership.
- v_freight had no latest-wins dedup; a second freight.discovered for the
  same freight fanned the grid out to eight rows. Fixed: dedup, and a lint
  rule that a freight is discovered once.
- previously_carried read v_first_success (any stack), so on a multi-stack
  stage a freight one stack ran satisfied the lookback. Fixed: requires
  v_first_carried (every stack), keeps the last-any-stack time for the
  window; unmet text says "never carried on every stack".
- v_live collapsed on a multi-stack stage between legs (v_carried NULL →
  no stable row → every observed service was drift), and read only the
  single representative in-flight leg. Fixed: v_live is per stack — one
  stable row per distinct carried-stack freight, one canary row per
  distinct open-leg freight not carried — with weights only in the
  single-stable/single-canary case. Pinned: the Tue 14:21:15 check and
  m_observe_between_legs.
- v_release_stage showed a lapsed approval as the standing one. Fixed:
  valid filter; the card says "approval of T lapsed".
- group_concat without ORDER BY in v_audit_decision and v_hold_active
  (the class smells #18 records). Fixed with ordered subqueries.
- A phase citing a step index past the declared steps silently dropped
  the step gate; a rollback request naming another program's freight was
  accepted as a candidate. Both are now lint errors.
- Doc drift: program.md said direction compares to what the stage
  *carries* (it compares to what it is *decided to*); smells #45 said two
  step-term types where three exist; smells #38's worked example was not
  reproducible (reworded on F417's boundary); README's pure-check count and
  multistack mutation list were stale; the trace summary's
  rolled_back_from was computed and checked but never rendered; the
  pin-set's partial cell lost its per-stack detail.
- Not pinned before, now pinned: the rollback block's not_held term as a
  blocker (m_hold_during_request).

Recorded as design smells, not changed (#52–#57)
------------------------------------------------
- A stage re-declared mid-rollout reinterprets the in-flight step index
  under the new declaration (latest declaration wins); the transition
  does not cite the declaration it was started under.
- The step-gate block keys on the declared step kind at the reported
  index, not on the reported phase.
- payments/prod's rollback block (auto, previously_carried only) admits a
  jump to any freight ever carried and ignores an active hold —
  downgraded by the refuter: the block does what it declares, and the
  fixture's story is the abort.
- A decided-but-never-enacted stage never shows drift and `pending`
  carries no age — downgraded: documented in #44 as the intent/enactment
  gap living in the status column.
- The lapsed-approval reason is prose composed in SQL with raw ISO
  timestamps; the renderer cannot reformat it.
- The policy's `rule` text is trusted, not re-derived at lint time.

Refuted (kept for the record)
-----------------------------
- "ts-vs-seq is used in several latest views" beyond the two demonstrated
  — the refuter showed lint's monotonic-ts rule makes ts and seq agree
  except on ties, which the two fixes above cover.
- "Approval lapse is direction-blind: a forward supersession voids a
  still-valid approval" — this is the documented rule (#33), deliberately.
- "v_live announces an in-flight freight as live before any weight" —
  the documented intent (m_no_weights pins it).
